01The problem

On a hospital ward, small record-keeping mistakes have real consequences: a temperature logged twice, a patient discharged a day too early, a bed assigned twice. CareTrack Ward encodes the ward's rules directly into the app, so the software blocks the mistakes instead of relying on people to catch them.

02Roles & rules

  • Nurses record one temperature per patient per day. A second reading is flagged as a duplicate.
  • Doctors log rounds, approve discharges (only when a patient is eligible) and record deaths.
  • Admins admit patients (admission is blocked once the ward hits capacity), change beds, discharge doctor-approved patients, remove duplicate readings and view the activity log.

03The discharge rule

  1. Look back three daysEach of the three full days before today must have a recorded temperature.
  2. Check for feverAll three readings must be below 37.5 °C, and there must be no fever today.
  3. Doctor approvesOnly an eligible patient can be approved for discharge.
  4. Re-check at dischargeEligibility is checked again when the admin discharges, in case anything changed in between.
Re-checking at the moment of action, not just at approval, is a small detail that prevents a real class of bugs.

04Engineering decisions

  • Zero build step: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Open index.html and it runs, and it deploys as a static site on Vercel.
  • Rules separated from UI: data.js holds demo data, constants and clinical rules, while app.js handles views, permissions and workflows.
  • Responsive by design: a sidebar on desktop, a bottom tab bar on phones, a slide-in drawer, and tables that turn into cards on small screens. Light and dark themes are included.
  • Persistent demo: state is saved in localStorage, with a "Reset demo" button to restore seed data.
