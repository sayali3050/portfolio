01The problem

On industrial sites, hazards often go unreported because reporting is slow, paper-based or only in English. Physical strain and repetitive work, the drudgery that causes worker fatigue, is even harder to see in the data.

SafeOps started from an ergonomic analysis of industrial work processes: find what causes fatigue, then design safer work practices through structured assessment. The platform turns that analysis into a tool workers and supervisors use every day.

02What I built

  • Mobile hazard & incident reporting in English, Hindi, Marathi and German.
  • CAPA workflow: supervisors investigate reports and close them with corrective and preventive actions.
  • Admin dashboards for risk, compliance and physical workload ("drudgery").
  • Risk scoring and an ergonomics questionnaire.
  • Training courses, PPE tracking and daily checklists.
  • A searchable knowledge base for company SOPs, plus analytics with CSV/PDF export.
  • A site-wide emergency alarm.

03Where the AI fits

  1. A worker writes a reportFree text, from a phone, in their own language.
  2. AI structures itThe model turns it into a structured report and classifies the hazard.
  3. AI suggests controlsIt proposes possible controls, clearly labelled as suggestions.
  4. A human decidesA supervisor reviews, investigates and closes the report through CAPA.
Humans stay in charge. In safety, a confident wrong answer is dangerous, so every AI output is shown as a suggestion and the final call always belongs to a person.

04Engineering decisions

  • Provider abstraction: the OpenAI API (chat and embeddings) sits behind an interface, so the model provider can be swapped without touching the rest of the app.
  • Offline Demo AI mode: the full app can be demoed with no API key or internet connection.
  • Security: JWT authentication, bcrypt password hashing and role-based access control for workers, supervisors and admins.
  • Typed end to end: TypeScript on the frontend, with Pydantic v2 and SQLAlchemy 2 (plus Alembic migrations) on the backend.
  • One-command setup: Docker Compose runs the app, PostgreSQL 16 and nginx together.
