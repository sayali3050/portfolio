02 / Final-year project · Full-stack + AI

            
SafeOps

            
An AI-assisted occupational health & safety platform.

            
Analyzes industrial work processes to find the causes of worker fatigue and drudgery, then recommends safer practices through structured ergonomic assessment. Workers report hazards from their phones in English, Hindi, Marathi or German. AI classifies hazards and suggests controls, always labelled as a suggestion so a human makes the call. Includes CAPA workflows, risk scoring, PPE tracking, analytics and a site-wide emergency alarm.

03 / Agentic AI · Support automation

            
TAM Triage Agent

            
An LLM agent that chooses its own tools to triage support tickets.

            
Resolves the account (even with a blank ID), gathers tier, renewal and usage context, retrieves policy articles, then decides category, priority, routing, escalation and duplicates. It drafts a customer reply and an internal note, with a full reasoning trace. Built for a dataset of 3,000 tickets, 200 accounts and 390 KB articles.

            
PythonTool callingOllamaAnthropic / OpenAISchema output
